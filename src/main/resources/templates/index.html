Why Choose Nabarangpur Pharmacy College?

Nabarangpur Pharmacy College is located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Established to provide quality Pharmacy education, our institution is affiliated with a reputed university and accredited by INC. We offer B.Sc, GNM, and M.Sc Pharmacy programs supported by highly qualified faculty and excellent infrastructure.

Our campus includes facilities such as a library, laboratories, hostel, computer center, canteen, and access to hospital training. We are committed to holistic Pharmacy education, community engagement, and research excellence.

World Class Facilities

JINS was inspected by INC in the year 2005 and permitted annual intake of 30 students for GNM program. 1st batch GNM was admitted in the year 2022-2023 academic session. In 2023 GNM seat was enhanced from 30 to 50 students. Thus 50 students were admitted in the 2023-2024 session. Basic B.sc,4 years degree program of 4 years was started from 2023-2024 session after approval of INC with annual intake of 40 students. In 2012-13 session 2 year Post Graduate Pharmacy degree was started with four specialties from the academic session 2015. The B.Sc Pharmacy seat was enhanced from 40-60 annual intake.
